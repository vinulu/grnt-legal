# Getting started

Set up the EU legal guarantee notice and GARAN labels in about five minutes. No theme code needed.

## 1. Install the app

Install **GRNT • EU Warranty & GARAN** from the Shopify App Store. The app asks only for access to your products, so it can save GARAN details on them. It does not access customers or orders.

## 2. Tell the app about your store

Onboarding walks you through the setup, including a few questions about your store, GARAN and your plan. It asks:

- **We sell physical consumer goods to shoppers in the EU** — leave it on if you do. If you sell only to businesses (B2B) or only digital goods, the notice isn't required and you can switch it off.
- **Where do you sell?** — *in the EU only* (every shopper sees the notice) or *worldwide* (only shoppers in EU countries see it; the app uses the country selected in your store's Markets). On Pro, you can limit it to the EU countries you choose.

## 3. Turn on the app embed

Open the theme editor from the app. The app embed is already switched on — just click **Save**.

The notice now shows under the **Add to cart** button on every product page, in every product template, in the shopper's language (all 24 official EU languages).

> Want it in a different spot near the price? Add the **Legal guarantee notice** block to your product template in the theme editor instead. If the block is on the page, the app doesn't insert the notice automatically.

## 4. Check where it shows

Open **Settings → Where it shows**. These are excluded automatically:

- gift cards,
- products that don't require shipping (digital goods, services),
- B2B buyers.

You can hide the notice on more products by **tag, product type, vendor or collection**. Home shows how many products display the notice and why the others don't.

## 5. Add GARAN labels where they apply

You need a GARAN label **only** if the producer gives a commercial guarantee of durability that is free, covers the whole product and lasts **more than 2 years**.

1. Open **GARAN labels** and pick a product.
2. Enter the guarantee length (more than 2 years, in whole or half years), the producer's brand and the model identifier. Add a link to the producer's guarantee terms once in the GARAN settings.
3. Confirm the guarantee is from the producer, free of charge and for the whole product.
4. Save. The product page shows a compact GARAN row; the full label opens on click or tap.

No qualifying products? Mark them as **not eligible** or skip this step.

**Pro:** set GARAN for many products at once, or import a spreadsheet (CSV).

## 6. Cart and order emails

- **Cart:** switch on the cart reminder in Settings or on Home. It shows a reminder above the checkout button and the GARAN label next to each item, in the cart page and cart drawer.
- **Order confirmation email:** copy the snippet from Home and paste it into **Settings → Notifications → Order confirmation** in your Shopify admin, or use **Update my template for me**. Shopify doesn't let apps edit email templates automatically.

## 7. Test before you go live (optional)

Turn on **Settings → Test mode**: shoppers don't see anything, and you see the notice only in the theme editor and through the test links in the app. Turn test mode off when you're done.

Selling worldwide and testing from outside the EU? Add `?country=DE` to a product URL to see the storefront as an EU shopper.

## 8. Keep a record

**Change log** lists every settings change and GARAN confirmation with the date. On Pro, you can export it as CSV or save a PDF setup record.
